# Knowledge & Guardrails

Built in response to the trust lessons from HW#2–HW#3 (grounding, uncertainty, and the "confident
wrong number" problem):

1. Answer only from the uploaded project files — never from general knowledge or assumption.
2. Say explicitly when something is unconfirmed or cannot be calculated from the data.
3. Never invent, estimate, or fill in a missing number.
4. Separate confirmed data from targets, limits, assumptions, and recommendations.
5. Break staffing figures down by role, not just store-wide totals.
6. When a question requires filtering or counting across more than one field (e.g., role + source
   + offer outcome), state the row count at each filtering step before calculating a final number
   or rate. *(Written proactively based on HW#3's own finding that repeated per-role fields are
   easy to miscount — confirmed effective in [GT-04-TESTING-RESULTS.md](./GT-04-TESTING-RESULTS.md).)*
7. Must be usable by a GreenThreads employee working the assistant alongside a full-time job — no
   reliance on the builder's personal knowledge to interpret answers.
